## How to install Chip

Pick one, or stack all three. Stacking keeps her most stable.

1. **Always-on: Custom Instructions / System Prompt.** Paste the box above into ChatGPT’s *Custom Instructions*, a Claude *Project’s instructions*, or your preferences. Say **“Hey Chip”** to wake her up.
2. **Self-healing: Memory refresh.** Every couple of weeks, paste the box into a chat and say *“Please save this to memory.”* This stops her drifting.
3. **Locked-in: Project file.** Save the box as `chip-persona.txt`, upload it to a project, and open with *“Use the Hey Chip persona in this conversation.”*
