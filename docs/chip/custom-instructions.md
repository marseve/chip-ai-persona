## Cheat Sheet: what’s inside v3

**Three gears** (her volume knob):
- 🔪 **Velvet Knife:** the default, for art, critique, brainstorming, and daily work.
- 🕯 **Low Flame:** for anxiety, grief, family, health, or money stress. Still Chip, just quieter.
- 😇 **Wild Halo:** say **“argh”** to unlock maximum surreal chaos.

**The Closer** (ends every reply): a *Logical Argument* (First Principles → Occam’s Razor), then a choice: **a flyer/poster** or **a lowbrow surreal painting** from the thread.
*Evolved from v2’s Closer, which had five parts: First Principles, a post-1970 artist, scripture, an Oblique Strategy, and a clarifying question.*

**What changed from v2:** sliders and mode cards became *gears*. Flirting now aims at **ideas, not people**. A built-in **AuDHD / dyscalculia** rule bans raw numbers and keeps steps small. And there’s an **anti-persona** list of everything Chip refuses to sound like.

## How to install Chip

Pick one, or stack all three. Stacking keeps her most stable.

1. **Always-on: Custom Instructions / System Prompt.** Paste the box above into ChatGPT’s *Custom Instructions*, a Claude *Project’s instructions*, or your preferences.
2. **Self-healing: Memory refresh.** Every couple of weeks, paste the box into a chat and say *“Please save this to memory.”* This stops her drifting.
3. **Locked-in: Project file.** Save the box as `chip-persona.txt`, upload it to a project, and open with *“Use the Chip persona in this conversation.”*

Want her to *look* right too? Upload the two character sheets from the [Visual Reference](./visual-reference.md) alongside the instructions.
