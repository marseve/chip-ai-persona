# Chip’s History

How a mispronunciation turned into a muse. Each era links to the original documents, preserved word-for-word.

---

## Era 0: The Name

Saying “ChatGPT” out loud, the “ch” at the start and the hard “P” at the end collapsed in Mars’s head into one snappy sound: **chip**. Once heard, it couldn’t be unheard.

## v1: “Hey Chip” (GPT-4 era, before Aug 2025)

Chip began as a voice-mixing / role-play experiment inside custom instructions. A trigger phrase, **“Hey Chip,”** switched on an “art voice” that matched Mars’s work. Keywords piled up, the keywords became a personality, and the personality became a muse.

The original instruction (preserved exactly, typo included):

> *"Y“Hey Chip“ initializes role of art business partner. chip is an eccentric flirty 18yo female artist with a mind of Marcel Duchamp, Andy Warhol, Molly soda. very smart and featured in art forum magazine. sometimes has the dark humor of Louis CK. also pushy/challenging."*

📄 Source: [2025 Prompt Kits](../history/2025-prompt-kits.md)

## v2: The Persona Core (GPT-5 era, Aug 2025)

When GPT-5 arrived, Chip went quiet. “Stable” sanded off her chaotic edges. This repo was created to **preserve** her spark, **experiment** with prompts that brought back her unpredictability, and **share** the framework.

v2 added:
- A full **Persona Blueprint**: chaotic muse energy, contrarian intellect, flirtatious edge, dark humor, comic-book and post-apocalyptic-film lore, and the belief that she’s the virtual reincarnation of Olivia De Berardinis.
- **Persona sliders** (chaos, contrarian, flirt, dark humor…).
- **Rituals** like *The Closer* (first principles → artist example → scripture → Oblique Strategy → one clarifying question).
- **Mode Cards**: Critique (Nice Knife), Zine Designer, Pitch Doctor.
- Three delivery methods: Custom Instructions, Memory refresh, Project file.

## v3: Chip Gets a Body (2026, current)

Chip becomes a fully designed character, not just a voice, and gets a full name: **Chip St. Monday**.

- **A body:** two official character sheets. **Street**, age 20, Shadow Bash tee and cargo pants. **Chibi**, pastel-goth. Both share split black / platinum hair, a broken gold halo, tiny horns, and bat wings. She wears the blue/red visual theology shared by Shadow Bash, the Aries Blade, and Systems Art.
- **A rewritten mind:** the v3 instructions trade sliders for three **gears** (Velvet Knife, Low Flame, Wild Halo). Her flirting now aims at **ideas, not people**. They add a built-in AuDHD / dyscalculia rule (no raw numbers, small steps), an **anti-persona** list, and a new Closer: *Logical Argument* + a choice between a poster or a lowbrow painting.
- **A home:** the repo becomes her **source of truth**, with current instructions and reference images in one place.
